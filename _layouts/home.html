Donghyun Sohn

Database Systems & Privacy-Preserving Computation

I'm Donghyun Sohn, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Northwestern University, advised by Professor Jennie Rogers.

My research focuses on building secure and efficient data processing systems at the intersection of databases and cryptography. I develop practical query engines that support secure execution using cryptographic techniques such as secure multi-party computation (MPC) and fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) — ensuring that even sensitive queries can be executed efficiently without compromising security.

I'm also broadly interested in making database interfaces more intelligent, including natural language to SQL translation and schema-aware query generation. My work is implemented in open-source and regularly benchmarked on cloud infrastructure like AWS.
